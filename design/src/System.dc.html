Radius and rhythm

full · pills
Block layout is a 4px scale: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 28 · 32, and every control and table row height is a multiple of four — controls 28 · 32 · 36, rows 36 · 40 · 48 · 52 · 56. Two things stay optical rather than snapped to the grid: horizontal padding inside a control, and the small gap between a mark and the label it belongs to (5–7px). The 2px step is for data marks and for anything nested in a 4px container, where the inner radius has to be tighter than the outer.
